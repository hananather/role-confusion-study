The authors add role signals throughout the context and fine-tune Llama 3 with those interventions. Their tests report improved resistance to many-shot jailbreaks relative to fine-tuning alone, with tradeoffs assessed on benign tasks. This is a trained role-signal approach, distinct from extracting a steering direction from an existing model.
