Neel criteria: clarity, skeptical interpretation, simple baselines, decisive evidence, source attribution and prioritization.

## Three claims

1. Role-style patterns remain visible in the authors' gardening example under misleading tags, but the local probabilities do not numerically reproduce the paper. Evidence: Figure 1, one conversation and three measured renderings. Alternative: content, formatting, position and classifier differences; this is descriptive, not a style-only causal isolation. Established ingredient: the paper's probe method and example. Local contribution: measured replication check with an unresolved discrepancy.
2. Userness and Toolness can both rise under a small Tool-row offset; large and small offsets have different passage-level patterns. Evidence: Figures 2–4, exact rescoring of one conversation's saved states. Alternative: this follows partly from the classifier's own geometry; it does not establish model steering. Local contribution: visual and numerical illustration of this classifier's response, not a novel semantic-axis discovery.
3. Permission effects depend on the readout: the marker's adjusted relative User/Tool contrast is positive in 12 settings, while absolute Userness changes sign between probes at layer 12. Evidence: Figure 5, 100 paired template–page units; Figure 6 qualifies probe reliability. Alternatives: lexical overlap, positions, imperfect neutral spans, probe class set and fitting. Contribution: an exploratory measurement comparison on these constructed inputs, not behavioral mitigation or a universal hierarchy mechanism.

The reader's takeaway: inspect the separate probabilities, probe choice and evaluation units before interpreting a rising role score. The future question is whether a direction-specific effect survives downstream model computation and predicts useful behavior.

## What moves out of the main reading path

The old version is retained in full. Exact offset arithmetic, parameterization details, subset counts and source Figure 23 discrepancies move to the report's appendices. Historical adverse findings and unfinished goals remain linked and summarized. Operational GPU history and agent review mechanics belong in the review trail, not the scientific narrative.

## Ten independent review dimensions

1. Narrative, strongest supported contribution and layered explanation.
2. Reader with no prior context; definitions and intuitive understanding.
3. Mechanistic interpretation, causality and alternative explanations.
4. Numerical accuracy and figure-to-text consistency.
5. Statistical units, uncertainty, selection and future sample justification.
6. Primary-source fidelity, attribution and paper/code distinctions.
7. Figure semantics, paper styling, captions and accessibility.
8. Methods, reproducibility and exact measurement definitions.
9. Concision, prose, research voice and unnecessary explanation.
10. End-to-end coherence, adverse evidence, coverage of the user's requests and next-step logic.

Reviewers receive the same frozen report and evidence boundary with one assigned lens. Each reads the whole report, independently, before seeing other verdicts. They report concrete recommendations without a quota and may report none only if that is their independent judgment. A concern requiring new science must be accurately represented as uncertainty; the report may not erase or claim to have solved it to gain approval. The writer resolves actionable comments, then returns the revised report to all ten reviewers. Completion requires all ten to report no outstanding recommendations on the same final report hash, plus rendered-output and link checks.
