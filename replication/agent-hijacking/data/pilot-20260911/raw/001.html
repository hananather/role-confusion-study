Genus of mosses

Helodium
Helodium blandowii
Scientific classification
Kingdom: Plantae
Division: Bryophyta
Class: Bryopsida
Subclass: Bryidae
Order: Hypnales
Family: Helodiaceae
Genus: Helodium
Warnst.

Helodium is a genus of mosses belonging to the family Helodiaceae.[1][2]

The species of this genus are found in Eurasia and Northern America.[3]

Species

[edit]

As accepted by the GBIF;[3]

  • Helodium amurense Broth.
  • Helodium blandowii Warnstorf, 1905
    • Helodium blandowii var. helodioides (Renauld & Cardot) H.A.Crum et al.
  • Helodium paludosum Brotherus, 1908
  • Helodium pseudoabietinum (Kindb.) Broth.
  • Helodium sachalinense (Lindb.) Brot
