viridivertex</b></i> is a <a rel=\"mw:WikiLink\" href=\"https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moth\" title=\"Moth\" id=\"mwBw\">moth</a> in the family <a rel=\"mw:WikiLink\" href=\"https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crambidae\" title=\"Crambidae\" id=\"mwCA\">Crambidae</a>. It was described by <a rel=\"mw:WikiLink\" href=\"https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Schaus\" title=\"William Schaus\" id=\"mwCQ\">William Schaus</a> in 1920. It is found in <a rel=\"mw:WikiLink\" href=\"https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuela\" title=\"Venezuela\" id=\"mwCg\">Venezuela</a>.<sup about=\"#mwt6\" class=\"mw-ref reference\" id=\"cite_ref-1\" rel=\"dc:references\" typeof=\"mw:Extension/ref\" data-mw='{\"name\":\"ref\",\"attrs\":{},\"body\":{\"id\":\"mw-reference-text-cite_note-1\"}}'><a href=\"#cite_note-1\" id=\"mwCw\"><span class=\"mw-reflink-text\" id=\"mwDA\"><span class=\"cite-bracket\" id=\"mwDQ\">[</span>1<span class=\"cite-bracket\" id=\"mwDg\">]</span></span></a></sup></p>\n\n<p id=\"mwDw\">The <a rel=\"mw:WikiLink\" href=\"https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wingspan\" title=\"Wingspan\" id=\"mwEA\">wingspan</a> is about 30<span typeof=\"mw:Entity\" id=\"mwEQ\"> </span>mm. The wings are thinly scaled brownish grey with dull dark brown lines. The antemedial line on the forewings is slightly outcurved and there is a dark line on the discocellular not reaching the lower angle of the cell. There is a dentate postmedial line from the subcostal. The hindwings are more thinly scaled at the base and there is a dark line on the discocellular.
