Norah Cecil Runge (1884–1978) was a British Conservative politician. She served as Member of Parliament for Rotherhithe from 1931 to 1935 and later became an alderman on the London County Council (1937–1961). During World I she was awarded an OBE for her work as superintendent of a soldiers’ and sailors’ free buffet at Paddington Station.<|return|>
